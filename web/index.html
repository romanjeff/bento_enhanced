bento_enhanced patcher

Make a bento_enhanced flashing kit for your Bento from your own copy of the official 1010music firmware. Everything happens in this page, on your device: your files are never uploaded.

Not affiliated with 1010music. Installing modified firmware is at your own risk and may affect your warranty; bento_enhanced is free software and comes with no warranty. The mod never touches the Bento's bootloader, which is what keeps the official way back to stock working. Please don't ask 1010music support about it.

Keep the kit private. It contains 1010music's firmware: it is for your own Bento only. Never share, upload or post it. This site contains none of 1010music's firmware: no run of 8 or more bytes from it, only short sequences any ARM code shares.

1 Your stock firmware

Download firmware 1.6.8, exactly that version, from 1010music's downloads page (Bento section). Then drop the downloaded zip here, or its two files bento1.bin and bento2.bin. File names don't matter: each file is recognised by its contents (its SHA-256 checksum), and anything but the exact official files is refused.

    Use BE4.47, not BE4.46: BE4.46 can sometimes reset-loop before the boot screen. BE4.47 removes the cause found, and no loop has been seen on it. To recover a looping BE4.46: unplug USB, hold power past the bootloader screen until the Bento switches off, leave it off a while, then power on.     3 Make the kit

    The patched bento1.bin has SHA-256

    • Download the kit : everything below, with a README and checksums.

    The kit holds 01_TEST_FIRMWARE/ (the mod's bento1.bin and your stock bento2.bin), 03_RESTORE_STOCK/ (your stock pair), README.txt, DEVICE_CHECKLIST.txt (the author's hardware test list: what changed and how to try it) and SHA256SUMS.txt. The folders keep the mod's bento1.bin apart from the stock one with the same name: copy from the right folder.

    4 Install

    1. Back up your SD card (copy the whole card to your computer).
    2. Copy both files from the kit's 01_TEST_FIRMWARE folder (bento1.bin and bento2.bin) to the root of the SD card, not into a folder, replacing any there. Eject the card properly.
    3. Put the card in the Bento. Long-press power to start the bootloader, then press REC to install. Don't switch the Bento off while it installs.
    4. The boot screen shows the version (for example BE4.47).

    On a phone or tablet you also need a way to copy files to the SD card (a card reader).

    Back to stock

    Do the same with the two files from 03_RESTORE_STOCK: that is the official firmware, installed the official way. Projects that use the mod's features are not supported on stock firmware: they usually open but may not play correctly. Keep a backup of any project you care about.

    How this works: the site holds, per release, the mod's own bytes plus instructions to copy everything else from your own stock files; the result is checked against the release's published SHA-256 before anything can be downloaded.
