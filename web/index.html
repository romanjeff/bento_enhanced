bento_enhanced patcher

Make a bento_enhanced flashing kit for your Bento from your own copy of the official 1010music firmware. Everything happens in this page, on your device: your files are never uploaded.

Not affiliated with 1010music. Installing modified firmware is at your own risk. The mod never touches the Bento's bootloader, so the official way back to stock always works. Please don't ask 1010music support about it.

Keep the kit private. It contains 1010music's firmware: it is for your own Bento only. Never share, upload or post it. This site holds only the mod's own bytes, never 1010music's.

1 Your stock firmware

Download firmware 1.6.8 from 1010music's downloads page (Bento section). Then drop the downloaded zip here, or its two files bento1.bin and bento2.bin. File names don't matter: each file is recognised by its SHA-256, and anything but the exact official files is refused.

    3 Make the kit

    The patched bento1.bin has SHA-256

    • Download the kit : everything below, with a README and checksums.
    • Download only bento1.bin : the patched file alone (install it with your stock bento2.bin).

    The kit holds 01_TEST_FIRMWARE/ (the mod's bento1.bin and your stock bento2.bin), 03_RESTORE_STOCK/ (your stock pair), README.txt, DEVICE_CHECKLIST.txt and SHA256SUMS.txt.

    4 Install

    1. Back up your SD card (copy the whole card to your computer).
    2. Copy both files from the kit's 01_TEST_FIRMWARE folder (bento1.bin and bento2.bin) to the root of the SD card, not into a folder, replacing any there. Eject the card properly.
    3. Put the card in the Bento. Long-press power to start the bootloader, then press REC to install.
    4. The boot screen shows the version (for example BE4.46).

    Back to stock

    Do the same with the two files from 03_RESTORE_STOCK: that is the official firmware, installed the official way. Projects that use the mod's features are not supported on stock firmware: they usually open but may not play correctly. Keep a backup of any project you care about.

    How this works: the site holds, per release, only the bytes the mod authored plus instructions to copy the rest from your own stock file; the result is checked against the published SHA-256 of the release before anything can be downloaded.
