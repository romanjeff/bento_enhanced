  01_TEST_FIRMWARE   bento1.bin = the test image on 1.6.8, bento2.bin = stock 1.6.8 Controller

Plan: docs\plan-p3-fm-bits.md, section "BE4.42". Everything below was measured in the emulator only. This is a delta on
BE4.41 (which you have run): only what changed is listed. Everything else on docs\checklist-v4.41.txt still applies
except its Bit Depth items (section 5, and Bit Depth in sections 2, 8, 9).

What changed since BE4.41 (your device feedback, 2026-11-16):
  - Bit Depth is gone: no Bit Depth knob on either oscillator row, no crush, no bits= in the black box. Slot 7 of an
    Analog row (where Bit Depth was) is empty, so FM 1>2 stays on knob 8 where you learned it.
  - FM is deeper. Sub FM and FM 1>2 follow a new curve: index = 16 x (knob)^1.5 (BE4.41: 8 x (knob)^2).
        knob     25 %   50 %   63 %   75 %   100 %
        BE4.41   0.5    2.0    3.2    4.5    8
        BE4.42   2.0    5.7    8.0   10.4   16
    So BE4.41's 100 % is now at about 63 %, BE4.41's 50 % is now at 25 %, and the top of the knob goes twice as deep.
  - A BE4.41 project with Bit Depth set opens normally; its Bit Depth value is ignored (plays as Full) and is dropped
    at the next save.
What gets worse (measured offline, read before section 4):
  - High notes at deep FM. The increment clamp (the oscillator cannot move more than 0.45 of a cycle per sample) now
    engages lower: FM 1>2 with OSC1 = OSC2 at 100 % from note 100 (E7, 2.6 kHz; BE4.41 from note 111, D#8), Sub FM
    (sub an octave down) at 100 % from note 111 (BE4.41 121). Above that the deepest swings are flattened.
  - Aliasing at 100 %: a sine -> sine FM 1>2 at 100 % is clean (below -70 dB) up to about C6 (note 84) but at C7 (note
    96) the inharmonic (aliased) energy is about -21 dB (BE4.41's 100 % there: -47 dB): expect audible inharmonic
    tones at the top of the knob on high notes. With a saw carrier it is 3 to 7 dB worse than BE4.41's 100 % at every
    note (for example -35 dB instead of -38 dB at C4).
  - CPU at 100 %: a full-FM voice costs about 2 to 3.5 % more instructions than BE4.41's 100 % (more edges to correct
    when the oscillator swings through zero further). At the same depth as before (63 %) it is slightly cheaper than
    BE4.41 (Bit Depth's check is gone). Bit Depth's own cost (about 4.5k instructions per voice block at 8 bits) is gone.
