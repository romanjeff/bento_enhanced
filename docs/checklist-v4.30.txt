bento_enhanced v4.30: the Kick drum synth on One-Shot pads (S6c, M7 part), on stock 1.6.8
=========================================================================================

Kit: flash\bento_enhanced-v4.30   boot label BE4.30
  01_TEST_FIRMWARE   bento1.bin = the test image on 1.6.8, bento2.bin = stock 1.6.8 Controller
  03_RESTORE_STOCK   stock 1.6.8 (bento1.bin, bento2.bin)
  Drum Synths BE     BE Kick.wav: the Kick's carrier (a 4801-frame, near-silent marker sample; our own file)
  SHA256SUMS.txt     hashes of every file in the kit (the carrier's too)
  manifest.json      what the image contains (groups, addresses, every write)
  DEVICE_CHECKLIST.txt  this file (docs\checklist-v4.30.txt)

In this image: everything BE4.22 has (docs\checklist-v4.22.txt), plus:
  - drums: a pad of a One-Shot track whose sample is BE Kick.wav plays DaisySP's synthetic bass drum (the Kick)
    instead of the file. The pad stays an ordinary one-shot pad: its velocity routes, filter, amp envelope, level,
    pan, sends, choke group, mute, Poly / Mono, tune, fine tune, LFO1, Note Mod, conditions and retriggers act on
    the Kick exactly as on a sample. Each native voice has its own Kick (a Poly pad rings several).
  - the black box has a DRUMS line (before the ANALOG line) with the Kick's CPU share and what the voices did.
NOT in it (S6c-2, the next kit): the drum page with the Kick's knobs (Tune, Decay, Tone, Accent, Sweep, Sweep Decay,
Dirt), their save / load, and a CPU readout on the page. Every Kick plays the default sound. Start, Length, Reverse
and Slice don't shape the Kick (row 15 of the plan): they are ignored, the Kick plays to its natural end.
Measured offline: one ringing Kick voice costs about 4 % of the audio block (a sample voice: 0.15 %). There is no cap
(decision D1): 8 Kick voices ringing at once on top of SPACED may overrun the block. Section 5 measures it.

0. Gate
[ ] Flash this kit ONLY after docs\checklist-v4.22.txt has passed on this unit. If BE4.22 is not done: stop here and
    bring its results first.
[ ] Back up the whole SD card on the computer (a folder copy of every file). Section 8 restores it.
[ ] Copy the kit's folder "Drum Synths BE" to the card, next to your other sample folders (anywhere the Bento's file
    browser reaches). Our firmware never writes this folder or reads the card for it.
[ ] Work on copies only: copy SPACED to SPACED_K (section 5) and make a new empty project KICK_T (sections 2-4, 6).
    Never load SPACED itself on this kit.

1. Boot and black box
[ ] Flash 01_TEST_FIRMWARE. The boot screen shows BE4.30.
[ ] Load KICK_T, save (nothing played). BENTO_SAVES.log: header "firmware BE4.30", a "# saved:" line, then
    "DRUMS blocks=0 ... starts=0 ... unbound=0 short_bufs=0" and the "ANALOG gen=N keys=ok ..." line as on BE4.22.
    If the DRUMS line says "area not taken": write down the ANALOG line too (it should say the same) and continue.
[ ] M7MEM "ext left=" = BE4.22's value (the drums use the same synth area). RAMCHK quiet, no "!!!", AUDIO rising.

2. The Kick on a One-Shot pad (KICK_T)
[ ] Track 1: One-Shot. In the file browser load "Drum Synths BE/BE Kick.wav" onto pad 1. Hit pad 1: a kick drum (a
    pitched thump with a short click), not silence. Hit it softly and hard. Save: the DRUMS line shows detects=1
    carriers=1 starts= the number of hits, blocks above 0, quiet_ends close to starts.
[ ] Load an ordinary sample (any drum hit) onto pad 2: it plays as on BE4.22. Hit pad 1 and pad 2 alternately and
    fast for 10 s: pad 1 is always the Kick, pad 2 always its sample (the bug the review found would have made the
    first hit of a voice silent, and could have let one pad's sound end up on the other).
[ ] Load the ordinary sample onto pad 1 (in place of the carrier): pad 1 now plays that sample. Load BE Kick.wav
    back onto pad 1: the Kick again.
[ ] Rename a copy of BE Kick.wav on the computer to "test.wav", load it onto pad 3: the Kick (the M7 recognises the
    file's content, not its name). (A sample that only has the name "BE Kick.wav" would play as a sample.)
[ ] Tail: let a Kick ring out at full volume with headphones: it decays smoothly to silence, no click at the end.

3. One-shot parity (each on the Kick pad, then on the sample pad for comparison; the two behave alike)
[ ] Tune +12 and -12, Fine +50: the Kick's pitch moves as a sample's would. LFO1 -> pitch: wobbles.
[ ] Filter cutoff down and resonance: audibly filters. Amp envelope decay short: the Kick is cut short (that is the
    pad's envelope, as on a sample).
[ ] Level and pan; a reverb / delay send: as on a sample pad.
[ ] Velocity: hit softly and hard with and without a Velocity -> Level route on the pad. Without the route: write
    down whether soft and hard hits differ in level (on the sample pad and on the Kick pad: they must behave the
    same). With the route: soft hits are quieter on both pads.
[ ] Sequence the Kick: a 1:2 condition and a NOT condition step; a 1/16 x4 retrigger: as on a sample pad.
[ ] Choke group: Kick pad and sample pad in one choke group: each cuts the other. Pad mute: the Kick is silent.
[ ] Poly pad, fast roll (8+ hits in a second): each hit is a new Kick ringing over the others. Mono pad: each hit
    stops the previous one (as a sample does).
[ ] Loop on (pad loop mode): the Kick plays once and then stays silent while the pad is held / the voice stays; no
    second Kick. Reverse on: the Kick plays normally (forward) and ends cleanly.
[ ] Note Mod on the track and recorded aftertouch with a Pressure -> Filter route: as on a sample pad.
[ ] Save: the DRUMS line's starts / retrigs / quiet_ends / unbound=0 / short_bufs=0. Write it down.

4. Gate: the Kick only on One-Shot tracks (D8)
[ ] Load BE Kick.wav onto a Multisample track, a Loop track, a Slicer track, a Granular track and a Shredder track,
    and play each: near silence on every one of them (stock plays the marker file, which is almost silent). No Kick.
    The DRUMS line's starts do not move (detects may).

5. CPU (SPACED_K)
[ ] Load SPACED_K, play it, save after 30 s: write down LOAD avg / bins / over-budget and the DRUMS line (the
    reference: no Kick playing, blocks=0).
[ ] Add a One-Shot track with the Kick on a Poly pad. Play SPACED_K and hit the Kick pad: 1 hit every 2 s for 30 s,
    save; then a roll of about 4 hits a second for 30 s, save; then 8 a second, save. After each save write down
    LOAD (avg, bins, over-budget blocks), AUDIO events - blocks, and the DRUMS line (avg_cyc, peak_cyc, share>=5%,
    >=15, >=30, voices_peak). Listen for glitches or timing slips while rolling.
[ ] No reset, no freeze at any point. If the audio glitches, note at which roll rate.

6. Save and load
[ ] KICK_T with the Kick pad: save, power cycle, load: pad 1 is still the Kick (the pad keeps its carrier sample).
[ ] project.xml on a computer: pad 1's sample is the carrier file; no bento_drum_* attributes yet (S6c-2).

7. Regression
[ ] An Analog track (BE4.22's checklist, section 6 items): plays, Sync / Glide act; the ANALOG line as on BE4.22.
[ ] An External track's note-0 pad still sends note 0 (BE4.22 section 2, first two items).
[ ] BE4.10 core items: curves, trigger conditions, a retrigger, live poly AT out of an External track, the hold
    shortcuts, strike velocity.

8. Rollback
[ ] BE4.22 (flash\bento_enhanced-v4.22\01_TEST_FIRMWARE): load KICK_T: no crash; the Kick pad is near silent (the
    marker file). Do not save there unless you mean to.
[ ] 03_RESTORE_STOCK (stock 1.6.8): KICK_T loads; the Kick pad is near silent.
[ ] Back to BE4.30 if you continue; restore the card backup from section 0 at the end.

Bring back: BENTO_SAVES.log (every save's DRUMS and ANALOG lines, the LOAD / AUDIO lines of section 5),
BENTO_CRASHES.log if any, and notes for every item that did not match (section 3's velocity item either way).
