bento_enhanced v4.01: the M7 features on stock 1.6.8 (sprint S3)
===============================================================

Kit: flash\bento_enhanced-v4.01   boot label BE4.01
  01_TEST_FIRMWARE   bento1.bin = the test image on 1.6.8, bento2.bin = stock 1.6.8 Controller
  03_RESTORE_STOCK   stock 1.6.8 (bento1.bin, bento2.bin)
  SHA256SUMS.txt     hashes of every file in the kit

In this image: the black box (as v4.00-bb168, stall/watchdog hooks not patched in) plus the M7 side of
  - the poly cap (External tracks, 6 voices),
  - the retrigger hooks, which also run curve playback (External and internal instruments),
  - the trigger-conditions engine (probability gate and loop counter),
  - the Note Mod engine (note on / note off / stop hooks).
NOT in it (S4/S5): step inspection and the per-step Condition / Retrig UI, strike velocity, hold
shortcuts, live poly AT and AT Out, the Mod Matrix and External mod rows, the probability clamps.

What can and cannot be heard in S3. Only the poly cap is fully active. The other engines are in the
audio path but get their data from M4 code that comes in S4/S5: the per-step word (retriggers, curve
handles, conditions) is loaded by be_step_v1, which stock 1.6.8 drops (S0), the curve pool is loaded by
the M4, and the Note Mod settings are published by the M4. So on BE4.01 SPACED's retriggers, curves,
per-step conditions and Note Mod do NOT play: that is expected. What this run checks for them is that
they are harmless in stock's path: the song plays exactly as on stock 1.6.8, no hanging or missing
notes, no crash. (Old conditions still stored in the probability byte, values 100..173 from before
v3.3 and never re-saved, do play their A:B: only if such a project exists.)

    section: logs readable, RAMCHK quiet, manual reset keeps the black box, cold boot, rollback to v3.72).
    If any of them failed or was skipped: STOP, do S2 first.
[ ] Full card backup.

    when opened again on v3.72. Every save below goes to a copy: load SPACED, Save As "SPACED_S3" once,
    and from then on load and save only SPACED_S3.
[ ] Stock 1.6.8 baseline of SPACED by ear (S0) is fresh in memory, or record it again now on stock 1.6.8.

2. SPACED regression, one feature at a time (play SPACED_S3 start to end; compare with stock 1.6.8)
[ ] Poly cap (External tracks). Play a passage with more than 6 overlapping notes on one External
    track; if SPACED has none, hold 7 or more pads on an External track. The oldest note is cut, the
    rest keep sounding, nothing hangs after release or after Stop.
    Log ("# saved:" line, save after playing): "| poly sent=0 budget_drops=0 stolen=S retrig=R swallowed=W".
      - stolen > 0 after the 7-note test: the cap works. stolen=0 there means the M7 state is off
        (the Region could not be reserved): bring the log.
      - after Stop, W should equal S + R (each stolen or retriggered voice owes one native Note Off,
        which is swallowed when it comes). W < S + R after Stop means an owed Note Off never came.
      - poly sent=0 and budget_drops=0: live poly AT is not in this build.
[ ] Curve playback on an External track (a track with recorded AT curves in SPACED). Expected on
    BE4.01: no aftertouch from the curves (see above). Log: "| curves started=0 sent=0 late=0 skipped=0".
    started or skipped > 0 means the M7 found a curve handle in a note: bring the log. No stray
    aftertouch, CC or pitch bend on the External synth.
[ ] Retriggers (SPACED's ratcheted steps). Expected: each plays as one hit, as on stock 1.6.8; no extra
    or hanging notes on internal and External tracks.
[ ] Trigger conditions (steps with A:B / 1ST / PRE). Expected: they play on every pass, as on stock
    1.6.8. Also play a clip that uses Play Count (1.6.8's new loop count) if SPACED has one: no change
    from stock.
[ ] Note Mod on an internal melodic track, a drum track and an External track (SPACED's Note Mod
    tracks). Expected: notes unshifted, as on stock 1.6.8. Every note ends: hold Stop mid-phrase,
    switch patterns, mute and unmute; no note keeps sounding. Note lengths sound as on stock 1.6.8
    (1.6.8 fixed where sequenced note-offs land in the audio block; our note-off hook keeps that).
[ ] Whole song twice, Stop / Start, tempo change, pattern changes: no crash, no restart, no card
    error, no crackle beyond the stock 1.6.8 baseline.
[ ] Save SPACED_S3 once more (not SPACED) and bring back BENTO_SAVES.log and BENTO_CRASHES.log.
    (BENTO_DIAG.log with the per-event VOIC records "lr 1 = retrigger, 2 = steal, 3 = swallowed" is
    not written by default.)
