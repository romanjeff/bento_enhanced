bento_enhanced v4.21: Analog (VA) oscillators on Wavetable tracks, on stock 1.6.8 (sprint S6a, after its review)
================================================================================================================

Kit: flash\bento_enhanced-v4.21   boot label BE4.21
  01_TEST_FIRMWARE   bento1.bin = the test image on 1.6.8, bento2.bin = stock 1.6.8 Controller
  03_RESTORE_STOCK   stock 1.6.8 (bento1.bin, bento2.bin)
  SHA256SUMS.txt     hashes of every file in the kit
  manifest.json      what the image contains (groups, addresses, every write)
  DEVICE_CHECKLIST.txt  this file (docs\checklist-v4.21.txt)
The v4.20 kit (flash\bento_enhanced-v4.20) is superseded and was never flashed: do not flash it.

In this image: everything BE4.10 has (docs\checklist-v4.10.txt), plus the analog group:
  - a 9th entry "Analog" in the instrument menu: a Wavetable track whose WT1 / WT2 play band-limited analog shapes
    instead of a wavetable. Position = shape: saw (0) -> square (middle) -> narrow 8 % pulse (full). The sub
    oscillator, filters, envelopes, LFOs and the mixer are stock's.
  - Glide on WT1's 4th encoder slot (0..1000 = 0..2 s), Sync (hard sync WT2 to WT1) on WT2's 4th slot, Analog tracks only.
  - a CPU indicator: on an Analog track WT1's label reads "Analog CPU nn%" (the M7's average load over the last
    half second or so), with " !" for 2 s after an audio block ran over its time. It shows the load; it limits nothing.
  - saved in project.xml / presets as bento_analog, bento_analog_sync, bento_analog_glide (stock ignores them).
  - one memory reservation of 48 KB at the top of the M7's SDRAM pool (BE4.10: 16 KB), taken right after boot: the
    32 KB synth area below the M1 Region, which keeps its address. If the pool ever has less than 1 MB to spare at
    that moment, only the Region (16 KB) is taken, as on v4.10, and Analog tracks stay silent that boot.
  - black box: an "ANALOG ..." line in BENTO_SAVES.log at every save (CPU figures, keys, tracks, why the area is not
    taken if it is not).
NOT in it: drum synths (S6c), the Mod Matrix (deferred), the pane drawing the analog wave (the pane graphic is stock's).
No voice cap and no voice dropping on Analog tracks (Jeff, 2026-11-08): too many voices may glitch or slip timing.

0. Gate
[ ] Flash this kit ONLY after docs\checklist-v4.10.txt has passed on this unit. If v4.10 is not done: stop here.
[ ] Back up the whole SD card on the computer (a folder copy of every file). Section 7 restores it.
[ ] Work on copies only: on a computer, copy SPACED to SPACED_A (for section 5) and make two new empty projects,
    ANALOG_T for sections 2-4 and PLAIN_T for 4b. Hash the project.xml files (sha256) and write the hashes down.
[ ] From the v4.10 run, write down BENTO_SAVES.log's "ext left=" (M7MEM line). Expected on BE4.21: that value
    minus 32768.

1. Boot and black box
[ ] Flash 01_TEST_FIRMWARE. The boot screen shows BE4.21.
[ ] Load ANALOG_T, save (no note played yet). BENTO_SAVES.log: header "firmware BE4.21"; a "# saved:" line; and a
    line "ANALOG gen=N keys=ok tracks=0 | blocks=0 ...". Write down the line. If it says "area not taken" or
    "keys=TAKEN": STOP and bring the log back (the reason is on the same line).
[ ] M7MEM "ext left=" = the v4.10 value - 32768 (see 0). Any other value: bring the log back.
[ ] RAMCHK quiet (as v4.10), no "!!!" lines, AUDIO blocks rising.
[ ] Region still alive (it shares the reservation): on an internal melodic track record a note with pad pressure in
    the piano roll, play it back: pressure plays (curves); CURVES line "sent" counts > 0 at the save.

2. Menu and the Analog track
[ ] On an empty track of ANALOG_T open the instrument menu: 9 entries, the last "Analog". Pick it.
    Expected: the track becomes a Wavetable track; its INST page shows the Wavetable page.
[ ] The two oscillator panes read "Analog" / "Saw > Square > Pulse" where a plain Wavetable track says "No WAV".
    Within a second WT1's "Analog" becomes "Analog CPU nn%". Write down where that text sits on the screen and
    the number while idle. (The pane graphic itself is stock's: note what it draws.)
[ ] WT1's 4th encoder slot shows "Glide", WT2's 4th slot shows "Sync" (Off / On). A plain Wavetable track
    (pick Wavetable from the menu on another track) has neither.
[ ] Glide, one encoder detent at a time from the middle of its range: the value goes up with every detent and down
    with every detent (none stuck). Write down how the value is shown (0..1000, 0.0..100.0, a time ...) and its maximum.
[ ] Pick "Analog" again on the Analog track: Sync and Glide keep their values.
[ ] Pick "Wavetable" on the Analog track: it is a plain Wavetable track again (no Glide / Sync, "No WAV").
    Pick "Analog" again for the next sections.

3. Sound (headphones or monitors; a tuner app for the first item)
[ ] Play note 60 (middle C) from a pad: the tuner reads about 261.6 Hz. An octave up: about 523 Hz.
[ ] Position from 0 to full: saw -> square (hollow) -> thin pulse (nasal). No clicks while turning slowly.
[ ] Turn WT1 level down, WT2 level up: WT2 alone sounds. Detune WT2 (its pitch knob) a fifth up, then Sync On:
    the tone turns metallic / harsh and its pitch follows WT1; Sync Off: two pitches again.
[ ] Glide 0 / 500 / 1000 (or the display's low / middle / top): play two notes an octave apart one after the other.
    0: jumps; middle: about a second's slide; top: about two seconds. The slide starts from the track's previous
    note, also across voices.
[ ] Glide and the sub: sub oscillator level up, Glide about a third of its range. Play fast overlapping notes
    (legato runs, new notes while the slide is still moving), then hold one note for 3 s: the sub is in tune with
    WT1 (same pitch class as before the runs; no lasting detune). Repeat with Glide 0.
[ ] Native parts act: filter cutoff / resonance, amp envelope (attack, release), LFO -> pitch, the sub oscillator
    level: each audibly changes the Analog sound as on a Wavetable track.
[ ] Hold a 10-note chord (two hands / latch): all 10 sound; no reset. The CPU indicator rises. Note any crackle.

    "ok=0" after notes played on the Analog track: bring the log back (the M7 <-> M4 handle link failed).
[ ] Power cycle. Load ANALOG_T: still Analog, Sync and Glide as saved, sound as before. ANALOG: "loads=1".
[ ] Autoload: make ANALOG_T the project the Bento opens at power on (load it last, power off, or however the Bento

[ ] Save a preset of the Analog track, load it onto another track: that track is Analog with the same Sync / Glide.
[ ] A plain Wavetable track with a WAV in ANALOG_T (load any WAV onto it): unchanged sound before / after.

[ ] Open ANALOG_T on a computer: project.xml has bento_analog="1" bento_analog_sync=.. bento_analog_glide=.. on the

4b. Plain Wavetable tracks after Analog ones (the M7 reuses its track objects)
[ ] In PLAIN_T put a WAV on a Wavetable track (no Analog track anywhere in PLAIN_T). Save.
[ ] Load ANALOG_T, play its Analog track. Then load PLAIN_T and play the Wavetable track: it plays its WAV, not a
    saw. Load ANALOG_T again: the Analog track is Analog.
[ ] In ANALOG_T change the Analog track to Sampler, then on another empty track pick Wavetable and load a WAV: it
    plays the WAV. (Pick Analog on the first track again afterwards if you continue.)

5. CPU spot check (the full measurement is S6b)
[ ] Load SPACED_A, add one Analog track (a free track), play SPACED_A with it: 1 held note, then 4, then 10.
    Write down the CPU indicator for each and whether a "!" shows. Listen for glitches. Save after each. Bring
    BENTO_SAVES.log back: its LOAD and ANALOG lines (avg / peak pm, share histogram, osc_peak, voices_peak,
    load_pm, over_with_analog) are the S6b starting point. A reset: bring BENTO_CRASHES.log.
[ ] Expected (emulator, not measured on the device): about 6,300 instructions per Analog voice per block on top
    of the native voice, so 10 voices = roughly 5-10 % of the CPU. LOAD avg on SPACED was 59-65 % on v4.10.
